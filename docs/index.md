## Powerful Management

TicketForge isn't just a bot, it's a full CRM for Discord.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   **__Data-Driven Decisions__**
    
    Visualize your support health with the **Guild Statistics** dashboard. Track ticket volume, response times, and peak hours to optimize your staffing.

-   **__Web Portal Applications__**
    
    Share direct links to your forms. Users can apply via a beautiful external web interface, with responses syncing directly to your dashboard and Discord server.

-   **__Audit Logs__**
    
    Never lose track of a change. The **Activity Log** provides a detailed timeline of every configuration update, submission review, and dashboard login.

-   **__CSAT & Analytics__**
    
    Gather CSAT scores (1-5 Stars) and feedback after every ticket. Use this data to identify your top-performing support agents.

</div>
