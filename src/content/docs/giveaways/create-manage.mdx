TicketForge separates your **default giveaway configuration** from **individual giveaways**, allowing you to reuse your server defaults while customizing each giveaway's prize, schedule, and overrides.

---

## 1. Creating a Giveaway (5-Step Wizard)

Click **Create Giveaway** from either **Giveaways > Configuration** or **Giveaways > Manage Giveaways** to launch the wizard.

![Create Giveaway Wizard](/images/giveaways/create_wizard.png)

### Step 1: Prize & Basics
* **Prize** *(Required)*: The reward title shown in the giveaway header (max **256 characters**).
* **Description** *(Optional)*: Additional details, rules, or instructions displayed below the prize (max **2,000 characters**).
* **Announcement Ping** *(Create only)*: Optionally select Discord roles to ping when the giveaway is first published (**up to 3 roles** on Free, **up to 10 roles** on Premium).

### Step 2: Schedule (End Condition)
Choose how the giveaway concludes:

| End Condition | Behavior | Limits |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Duration** | Ends automatically after a set duration from publish time (or from now when editing). | `1 minute` – `365 days` (`525,600` mins) |
| **Specific Date** | Ends at an exact calendar date and time in your local timezone. | Must be at least 1 minute in the future |
| **Participant Goal** | Ends immediately once a target number of entries is reached. | `1` – `1,000,000` entries |
| **Server Member Goal** | Ends automatically when your Discord server reaches the target member count. | `2` – `10,000,000` members |

### Step 3: Winners
* **Number of Winners:** Set how many winners to draw (`1` – `50`).
* **Winner Reward Roles (Optional):** Pre-filled from your default configuration; override per giveaway if needed.

### Step 4: Access Overrides
Pre-filled from your default **Entry Requirements**, you can customize **Allowed Entry Roles**, **Blocked Entry Roles**, **Requirement Bypass Roles**, **Minimum Account Age**, **Minimum Time in Server**, and whether **Boosted Roles Extra Entries** apply to this giveaway.

### Step 5: Review & Publish
Review the full summary alongside a live Discord message preview, select your **Target Channel** (text or announcement channels where the bot has view & send permissions), and click **Publish Giveaway**.

---

## 2. Managing Giveaways

Navigate to **Giveaways > Manage Giveaways** to view, filter, and control all active, ended, and cancelled giveaways in your server.

![Manage Giveaways Inbox](/images/giveaways/manage_list.png)

### Search & Filtering
* **Search Bar:** Search by **prize name**, **giveaway ID**, or **host username**.
* **Channel Filter:** Filter giveaways by their Discord channel.
* **Status Pills:** Filter by `All Statuses`, `Active`, `Drawing`, `Ended`, or `Cancelled`.
* **Date Range:** Filter by preset windows or custom date ranges *(Custom ranges require Premium)*.

---

## 3. Giveaway Details & Lifecycle Actions

Click any giveaway card (or use its `⋮` action menu) to inspect live entries, host info, drawn winners, claim statuses, and access rules.

![Giveaway Details Modal](/images/giveaways/details_modal.png)

| Action | Available When | Behavior |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Edit Giveaway** | `ACTIVE` | Reopens the 5-step wizard to update prize, description, schedule, winner count, or access overrides and updates the live Discord message. *(Channel cannot be changed while editing.)* |
| **Repost to Discord** | `ACTIVE` | Posts a fresh giveaway message to the current or a new target channel without re-pinging roles, and removes the previous message if it still exists. Subject to a **30-second cooldown**. |
| **End Early & Draw** | `ACTIVE` | Immediately closes entries, draws eligible winners, assigns Winner Roles, and dispatches winner notifications. |
| **Cancel Giveaway** | `ACTIVE` | Cancels the giveaway without drawing winners and disables the Participate button in Discord. |
| **Reroll Winners** | `ENDED` | Draws `1` up to `winnerCount` new replacement winners. **Previous winners are automatically excluded** from being drawn again. |
| **Delete** | Any status | Permanently deletes the giveaway record and all participant entries. **Cannot be undone.** |
