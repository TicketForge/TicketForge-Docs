Protect your giveaways against alts, bots, and drive-by accounts while giving winners a seamless way to claim their rewards.

Navigate to **Giveaways > Configuration > Requirements & Claiming** to configure your global defaults.

![Requirements & Claiming Settings](/images/giveaways/requirements_claiming.png)

---

## 1. Role Eligibility & Winner Rewards

| Role Group | Purpose | Free Limit | Premium Limit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Required Roles** | Members must have at least one of these roles to enter. | Up to `5` | Up to `25` |
| **Blocked Roles** | Members with any of these roles are barred from entering. | Up to `5` | Up to `25` |
| **Bypass Roles** | Members with any of these roles bypass role, account age, and server tenure requirements. | Up to `5` | Up to `25` |
| **Winner Roles** | Automatically assigned to members when they win a giveaway. | Up to `3` | Up to `15` |

:::caution[Role Hierarchy Check]
Because the bot assigns **Winner Roles** automatically when drawing winners, the **TicketForge** bot role must sit **higher** in your Discord Server Settings than any selected Winner Role.
:::

---

## 2. Account & Membership Checks

* **Remove Entry When Leaving Server:** Automatically disqualifies participants who leave your Discord server before winners are drawn.
* **Minimum Account Age:** Require Discord accounts to be older than a configured duration (`0` = disabled, up to `10 years`).
* **Minimum Time in Server:** Require members to have been in your server for a minimum duration (`0` = disabled, up to `10 years`).

---

## 3. Captcha Verification Modes

Choose how participants verify they are human when clicking **Participate**:

| Mode | Behavior | Tier |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Disabled** | Members enter immediately upon passing role and account checks. | **Free** |
| **Web Verification** | Sends the user to the browser portal to solve an **hCaptcha** challenge with network reputation and device fingerprint checks. | **Free** |
| **Discord Captcha** | Presents an interactive visual image captcha solved directly inside Discord. | <Badge text="Premium" variant="caution" /> |

---

## 4. Prize Claiming

Link your giveaway system directly to **TicketForge Tickets** so winners can claim rewards cleanly:

* **Ticket Panel:** Select any ticket panel on your server. When a giveaway ends, winners receive a **Claim** button that opens a ticket under the selected panel.
* **Claim Time Limit** <Badge text="Premium" variant="caution" />: Set a deadline window (`0` = no limit, up to `365 days`) for winners to click Claim after the giveaway ends.

---

## 5. Giveaway Page & Public Web Portal

Every giveaway has a dedicated web page featuring live countdowns, goal progress bars, prize details, and winner announcements.

![Public Giveaway Portal](/images/giveaways/public_portal.png)

Control how your Discord giveaway message links to this page under **Giveaway Page**:

| Mode | Behavior |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Link the Prize** *(Default)* | Turns the prize title in your Discord embed or V2 block into a clickable hyperlink to the giveaway page. |
| **Link Button** | Attaches a **View Giveaway** URL button next to the Participate button. |
| **Disabled** | Hides all links and keeps the public giveaway page private (`403 Private Giveaway`). |

:::note[Web Captcha Portal Access]
Even if **Giveaway Page** is set to **Disabled**, members entering a giveaway that uses **Web Verification** still receive a private signed session link (`?t=`) so they can complete the hCaptcha and confirm their entry.
:::
