## How to Upgrade

1. Log in to the [TicketForge Dashboard](https://ticketforge.xyz/).
2. Select your server and navigate to the **Premium** tab in the sidebar.
3. Select **Monthly** or **Yearly** (Save 20%) and proceed to checkout.

:::tip[Vote to Earn Free Tokens]
You can earn temporary Premium access by running `/vote` in your Discord server and voting on Top.gg. Tokens accumulate daily to keep Premium active without a card.
:::
