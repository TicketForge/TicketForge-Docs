TicketForge includes an enterprise-grade member verification suite designed to gate server access, detect alt accounts, block proxies, and stop automated raid attacks.

New members are assigned an unverified role upon joining and must complete a verification check before unlocking your community.

## Verification Methods

TicketForge supports three verification methods to match your server's security posture:

| Method | Environment | Threat & Telemetry Engine | Challenge Type | Tier |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Web Verification** | Web Browser (`ticketforge.xyz/verify`) | **Full Suite** (VPN, Tor, DevTools, Alts) | hCaptcha or Top.gg Vote | **Free** |
| **Discord Captcha** | Native in Discord | Gateway signals | Visual image puzzle | <Badge text="Premium" variant="caution" /> |
| **Click to Pass** | Native in Discord | Account age filters | Single button confirmation | <Badge text="Premium" variant="caution" /> |

## 1. Quickstart Deployment Guide

### Step 1: Configure Role Hierarchy
In Discord, open **Server Settings > Roles** and make sure the **TicketForge** bot role is higher in the list than your Unverified role, your Verified member role, and any Quarantine roles.

:::caution[Permission Hierarchy]
Discord strictly blocks bots from assigning, revoking, or managing any role that is positioned equal to or higher than the bot's own highest role.
:::

### Step 2: Configure Roles in the Dashboard
1. Open the [TicketForge Dashboard](https://ticketforge.xyz).
2. Go to **Verification > General & Access**.
3. Under **Role Management**, configure your roles:
   * **Unverified Roles:** Assigned automatically when someone joins your server.
   * **Verified Roles:** Granted immediately once a user passes verification.

![Role Configuration](/images/verification/roles_setup.png)

### Step 3: Dispatch the Verification Panel
1. Click **Send to Discord** in the top action toolbar.
2. Select your designated `#verification` or `#welcome` channel.
3. The bot posts the interactive verification embed equipped with the **Verify** action button.

:::tip[Updating Existing Panels]
If you modify embed styling or button labels later, click **Update Message** in the action bar and enter the existing Discord message link to push changes without reposting.
:::

## 2. Core Gatekeeper Policies

Under **Verification > General & Access**, you can apply entry conditions before a user can even initiate verification:

* **Minimum Account Age:** Reject brand-new throwaway Discord accounts by setting a minimum account age requirement (configurable from minutes up to days or months). Accounts created more recently than the threshold are blocked.
* **Avatar Requirement:** Require users to have a custom Discord profile avatar. Users using default blank Discord avatars will be prevented from verifying until they set a profile picture.

## 3. The Public Verification Experience

When a user clicks the **Verify** button inside Discord using **Web Verification**:

![Web Verification Portal](/images/verification/verify_portal.png)

1. **Private Ephemeral Link:** The bot replies with a private, signed one-time link (`ticketforge.xyz/verify/:guildId?t=:token`).
2. **Device & Network Inspection:** The browser loads the verification portal and silently evaluates browser telemetry, network nodes, and headless automation drivers.
3. **Challenge Resolution:**
   * **hCaptcha Challenge:** The member solves a fast image puzzle.
   * **Verify by Voting:** Members can alternatively verify instantly by casting a vote for TicketForge on Top.gg.
4. **Instant Role Grant:** Once passed, the bot revokes their Unverified role and adds their Verified role in Discord in milliseconds.
