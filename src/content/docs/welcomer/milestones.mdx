Celebrate your community's growth automatically whenever your server hits major member count targets.

Navigate to **Welcomer > Milestones** and toggle **Milestone Celebrations** ON to configure your triggers and design.

![Member Milestones Trigger & Delivery](/images/welcomer/milestones_settings.png)

---

## 1. Trigger & Delivery

* **Announcement Channel:** Select the Discord text or announcement channel where milestone celebrations will be posted.
* **Trigger Thresholds:** Configure one or more member count targets (all thresholds must be at least **10 members**):

| Mode | Behavior | Examples | Tier |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Every X Members (Interval)** | Triggers automatically on every multiple of the configured number (up to 5 interval rules). | `100` fires at `100`, `200`, `300`, `400`... | <Badge text="Premium" variant="caution" /> |
| **Specific Numbers** | Triggers once when the server reaches the exact member count configured. | `50`, `250`, `1000`, `5000` | **Free** *(Expanded limit on Premium)* |

:::note[Milestone Evaluation Order]
If **Wait for Server Verification** is enabled on your server, welcome messages wait until verification passes—but **Member Milestones** are evaluated immediately when the member joins so your live member count matches the exact milestone trigger.
:::

## 2. Card Badge & Overlay

Milestone cards automatically feature a celebratory diamond-divider badge (in **Centered** and **Custom** layouts) or a frosted capsule pill (in **Horizontal** layout):

![Milestone Card Badge & Overlay](/images/welcomer/milestones_badge.png)

* **Badge Banner Text:** Customize the badge text (up to **32 characters**, default: `🎉 {number} MEMBERS`). Supports `{number}` and `{count}` placeholders.
* **Badge Accent Color:** Set a custom hex color (`#FBBF24` by default) applied to the badge text, diamond decorators, and gradient divider lines.

### Independent Milestone Card Design
* **Use Welcome Card Background (Enabled by default):** Inherits the exact layout, background theme, and dim opacity of your primary Welcome Card.
* **Independent Design (Disabled):** Reveals an embedded **Milestone Card Customizer** allowing you to design a completely separate layout, background theme, dim opacity, and drag-and-drop badge position (`milestoneBadgeX` / `milestoneBadgeY`) exclusively for milestone announcements.

## 3. Announcement Message

Customize the celebration message using the **Canvas Editor** at the bottom of the page:

![Milestone Announcement Preview](/images/welcomer/milestones_preview.png)

* **Embed / V2 Integration:** When using V1 embeds, `attachment://milestone.png` is automatically bound into the embed image slot. When using **Components (V2)** <Badge text="Premium" variant="caution" />, the milestone card renders directly inside the card block slot.
* **Supported Variables:** Use `{milestone.value}`, `{number}`, or `{count}` alongside `{user.mention}` and `{guild.name}` to shout out the member who pushed your server over the milestone.
